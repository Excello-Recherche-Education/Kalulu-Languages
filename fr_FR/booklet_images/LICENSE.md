Look & Learn image of its own (`BookletImages` in `language.db`, schema version
5). Only the booklets print them: the game never shows them, and they are left
out of the pack ZIP it downloads. Every file here is listed below, with where it
comes from and under what terms; the pack validator warns about one that is not
(rule 10 in `SCHEMA.md`).
